# Dataset coverage

Generated by `scripts/coverage_summary.py`.

## OSFI data

First and last fiscal quarter in `data/osfi/` (number of quarters). Only OSFI-regulated companies appear; iA, Desjardins, Beneva, Humania and UV report to the AMF and Assumption Life to the FCNB.

Breaks at the 2023 switch to IFRS 17 (fiscal 2024 for October year ends):

- **Portfolio mix** (`portfolio_mix.csv`): 2020 to 2022 from the IFRS 4 return (10Q), 2023 on from LF1 and LF2. The main lines (cash, bonds, mortgages, shares, real estate, segregated funds, total assets) line up across the break. Policy loans exist only before 2023, because IFRS 17 moved them into insurance contract balances. The bond split by issuer and by investment grade exists only from 2023 (`invested_assets.csv`).
- **Discount curves** exist only from 2023. IFRS 4 used CALM, which has no published discount curve, so the earlier blank is not a gap to fill.
- **LICAT** runs back to 2018 without a gap, but the 2023 LICAT revision changed available capital for IFRS 17 (the contractual service margin now counts), so ratios before and after 2023 are not like for like.
- BMO Life and RBC Life have no IFRS 4 rows for fiscal 2023 because their October year end put the switch at November 2023.
